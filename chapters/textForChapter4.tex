For the turbulent wind case, the rotor-effective wind filters of Section~\ref{subsec:ResultsqLPVModel} are used, since a single-point wind speed acting on the whole rotor overestimates the high-frequency excitation of the turbine. The plant Mdl2 is driven by the filtered wind speeds $V_{\text{filt},\tau}$ and $V_{\text{filt},T}$ in closed loop with both the PI baseline controller and the qLMPC, so that both controllers are compared on the same plant. In addition, the qLMPC is scheduled with the filtered wind speed $V_{\text{filt},\tau}$, which also drives the aerodynamic torque of the plant. The qLMPC therefore has perfect knowledge of the rotor-effective wind speed, in line with the best-case character of this study; in practice, this wind speed would have to be estimated. In the stepped wind sweep, the wind changes uniformly over the whole rotor disk, so the filters are not used and both controllers receive the freestream wind speed $V_\infty$ directly.
