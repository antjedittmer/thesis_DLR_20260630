tower top fore-aft acceleration replaces the tower base DEL, no blade and tower top DELs are given, and $C_\text{Act}$ is calculated with a pitch moment estimated from the pitch angle, its derivatives, and the wind
speed, fitted by least squares to the pitch moment of blade~1 in the FAST runs of the PI and the notch filter design at
8\textdegree/s. The fit explains only a quarter of the variance of the pitch moment ($R^2 = 0.25$); most of the
remaining variance lies around the rotational frequency 1P, which depends on the blade azimuth and cannot be
represented by the collective signals of Mdl2. The actuator power is nevertheless reproduced within 0.3\% for the
qLMPC designs on FAST, as the pitch moment is dominated by its nearly constant mean value of about $-86$\,kNm,
which is the same for all controllers. $C_\text{Act}$ is therefore essentially the ratio of the mean absolute
pitch rates: on FAST, the two differ by less than 2\%, and for the qLMPC on Mdl2 by less than 0.5\%.
